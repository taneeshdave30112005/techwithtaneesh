🧠 Why This Matters

For users and businesses, a move to Windows 12 (or whatever the next major release is called) could mean:

  • Better support for AI workflows and assistants built into the OS.
  • Improved multitasking and productivity via redesigned UI and device integration.
  • Potentially stricter hardware requirements — meaning some older PCs may not support the upgrade.

📝 What Should You Do Now?

  1. Check your current PC’s compatibility — if you’re on Windows 11 and hardware is newer than ~3-4 years, you’re likely ok.
  2. Back up your important data and keep your system updated — once a major new OS arrives, upgrades may become mandatory or more disruptive.
  3. Don’t rush to upgrade — given that nothing is confirmed yet, you could wait for official announcements and reviews before switching.
